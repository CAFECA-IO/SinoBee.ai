This Refund Policy explains when and how paid services on {{site}} are refunded. It is part of the [Terms of Service](/terms). The Traditional Chinese version prevails if the two versions differ.

## 1. The 7-day cooling-off right

The Service is a digital service delivered online and starts as soon as you pay and agree. Under Article 2 of Taiwan's rules on reasonable exceptions to the withdrawal right in distance sales, such services are not subject to the 7-day unconditional withdrawal right of Article 19 of the Consumer Protection Act. We still refund in the cases below.

## 2. Subscriptions

| Situation | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| Within 7 days of payment, and the period has not started (a prepaid period queued after your current one) | Full |
| Within 7 days of payment, the period has started but no subscription quota was used | Full |
| The period has started and quota was used | No refund, and no pro-rata refund by days |
| An outage attributable to us made the period unusable | Proportional to the affected time or quota |

- A refunded period is cancelled at once; later prepaid periods move up.
- Subscriptions never renew automatically, so there are no renewals you did not approve.

## 3. Commissioned tasks and evaluations

| Situation | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| Cancelled before the task started running | Full budget |
| Unused budget after the task completes, is closed or fails | The unused part (budget minus charges, rounded down to whole TWD) |
| The task failed because of a platform error | The charges for that task |

- Charges for work actually performed (model tokens, VM time, storage) are not refunded unless caused by our error.
- A task may have several payments (budget and top-ups); refunds are made per payment and never exceed the unused budget in total.


### Crowdfunded tasks

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Proposal fee | Non-refundable; it becomes credit for the spec dialogue tokens and reference file storage. Credit left when the campaign is funded joins the task budget |
| You withdraw a pledge during the campaign, or the campaign misses its goal | Cards are not charged; points or subscription quota used are returned in full |
| The goal is reached but the charges collected fall short during the charging grace period | Charged cards are refunded in full; points and quota are returned |
| Budget left after the task is completed, closed or failed | Returned to the backers as platform points in proportion to their pledges (quota pledges get quota back); no cash refund |
| A disputed review where the platform decides the result does not meet the spec | The whole budget, spent part included, goes back to the backers as points |
| The task failed because of a platform error | Platform points by default; a backer may ask for a refund to the original payment method instead |

- Platform points: 1 point equals NT$1, usable only for crowdfunding pledges, not transferable, never paid out in cash, and they do not expire. Unused points are handled according to law if your account or the Service ends.
- If the spec is revised during a campaign, nothing is charged for 72 hours after the revision and backers who disagree can withdraw.

## 5. How refunds are paid

1. Refunds always go back to the original payment method, through our payment provider Oen (應援科技股份有限公司, [https://oen.tw/](https://oen.tw/)):
   - Card, Apple Pay, LINE Pay: refunded immediately; it usually takes 7–14 business days to appear, depending on your bank or wallet.
   - Paid convenience-store codes or ATM transfers: we need your bank account details and Oen transfers the money.
2. Under Oen's rules **each payment can be refunded only once**; after a partial refund the rest cannot be refunded.
3. Oen issues the matching allowance for the e-invoice.

## 6. No refund

- Accounts suspended or terminated for breaching the [Terms](/terms) (for example sharing accounts, circumventing quotas or abuse).
- Services already used or usage already charged, except as provided in sections 2 and 3.

## 7. Changes

We may change this policy and will announce the effective date on the site; payments made before a change follow the policy in force when you paid.
