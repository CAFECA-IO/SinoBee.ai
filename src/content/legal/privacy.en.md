## 4. How long we keep it

- Account data: while the account exists, then as long as the law requires.
- Payment and invoice records: as tax and accounting law requires (at least 5 years).
- Results and containers: while subscribed plus a {{graceDays}}-day grace period; without a subscription they expire {{freeDays}} days after a task finishes.
- Shared content: until you revoke it or it is taken down; on takedown the content key is destroyed so the encrypted data can no longer be read.
- Usage logs: up to 1 year, longer when needed (for example to investigate abuse).

## 5. Your rights

Under Article 3 of the Personal Data Protection Act you may ask us to:

1. let you inquire about or review your data;
2. give you a copy;
3. supplement or correct it;
4. stop collecting, processing or using it;
5. delete it.

Contact us as described in section 8. Some data must be kept by law (for example invoices), and deleting some data means we can no longer provide the Service; we will explain this in our reply.

## 6. Security

We protect data with HTTPS, access controls, least privilege and encrypted storage (private tool connections are kept only as ciphertext). No transmission over the internet is perfectly secure; if a personal data breach occurs we will notify you as the law requires.
